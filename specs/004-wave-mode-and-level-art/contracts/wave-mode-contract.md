# Contract: Wave Mode Player-Visible Guarantees

- **Deploy**: the hideout DEPLOY tab shows a "Rec Center Showdown (Wave Mode)" tile with the best
  wave and the owned blasters. It is free to enter.
- **Controls (Wave Mode)**: LMB fire (hold for automatic blasters and the soaker), RMB aim, R
  reload, 1-5 / mouse wheel switch blasters, Esc pause. Movement is the same as in raids.
- **HUD**: wave number, adults remaining or the intermission countdown, ammo as `mag / reserve`
  (∞ for the pistol), a reload bar, KOs and cash earned this run, a center banner for wave
  start/clear/boss, and a boss health bar during boss waves. Heat, binder and timer are hidden.
- **Feedback**: every shot has a muzzle flash, recoil, a sound and a visible projectile or stream;
  every hit has an impact effect and a hit marker; headshots show "BONK!".
- **Safety**: no shot, splash or projectile can affect a kid. Spectator kids cheer from the
  bleachers behind a net.
- **Adults**: surround, weave when aimed at, may sidestep after a hit, recover when stuck. Ranged
  adults wind up visibly before throwing. The boss telegraphs its megaphone blast for 1 s.
- **End of run**: a knockout or "Quit run" in the pause menu ends the run. The hideout report shows
  the wave reached, KOs, cash earned and NEW BEST if beaten. Nothing owned is lost.
- **Shop**: a BLASTERS section lists the 5 blasters with stat bars and prices; the pistol is free.
