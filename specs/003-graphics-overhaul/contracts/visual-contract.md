# Contract: Visual Guarantees

These are player-visible guarantees that tests and screenshot reviews check.

- **Characters**: outlined at High (every body part mesh has the outline overlay), never at Low.
  Banded shading is always on.
- **Faces**: eye whites + pupils, nose and mouth on every character. Cheeks on kids.
- **Expressions**: adults are angry within 0.5 s of spotting the player and neutral again when
  patrolling or searching. Kids are sad while crying or scammed.
- **Textures**: Playground (grass, asphalt), Locals (asphalt, wood floor, brick), Pizza (tiles,
  carpet), Mall (tiles, concrete).
- **Moods**: Playground noon, Locals golden hour, Pizza party, Mall cool daylight, hub default.
- **Effects**: sparks on hit, stars over stunned and KO'd adults, tears on crying kids, sparkles on
  the whale, dust indoors, pulsing extract beams.
- **Settings**: a "Graphics quality: High / Low" toggle. The note "Applies from the next raid" is
  shown, and the choice persists.
- **First-person arms**: toon-shaded, never outlined.
